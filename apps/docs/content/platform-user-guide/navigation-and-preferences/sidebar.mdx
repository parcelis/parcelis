---

The sidebar includes links for Portfolio, Properties, Leases, Tenants, Maintenance, Income, and Settings.
Portfolio, Properties, Tenants, Maintenance, Income, and Settings are active areas in the current app. Leases is
visible as a planned product area.

The expanded sidebar displays the Parcelis banner for the current theme and the active organization's avatar. If you
can access more than one organization, an Organization selector appears above the navigation; selecting an
organization switches the workspace and its operational records.

Collapsing the sidebar switches to the Parcelis mark while preserving access to navigation and theme controls. Hover
over the collapsed sidebar to temporarily expand it. The organization selector and avatar are available when the
sidebar is expanded.

## My Account menu

Select **My Account** at the bottom of the sidebar to access account and community resources. The menu includes:

- **Change password**, which requires your current password and matching new-password entries of at least 12 characters.
- **Documentation Site**, **GitHub**, **Discord Community**, and **Request a feature** links.
- **Sign out** to revoke the current browser session.
- Theme controls at the bottom of the menu.
