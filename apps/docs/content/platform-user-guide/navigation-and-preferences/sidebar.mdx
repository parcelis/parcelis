title: Sidebar
---

The sidebar includes links for Portfolio, Properties, Leases, Tenants, Maintenance, Accounting, and Settings.
Portfolio, Properties, Tenants, Maintenance, and Settings are active areas in the current app. Leases and Accounting
are visible as planned product areas.

The expanded sidebar displays the Parcelis banner for the current theme. Collapsing the sidebar switches to the
Parcelis mark while preserving access to navigation and theme controls.
