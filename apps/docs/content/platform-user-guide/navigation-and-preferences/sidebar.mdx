id: sidebar
title: Sidebar
---

The sidebar includes links for Portfolio, Properties, Leases, Tenants, Maintenance, Income, and Settings.
Portfolio, Properties, Tenants, Maintenance, Income, and Settings are active areas in the current app. Leases is
visible as a planned product area.

The expanded sidebar displays the Parcelis banner and the active organization's avatar. If you can access more than
one organization, an Organization selector appears above the navigation; selecting an organization switches the
workspace and its operational records.

Collapsing the sidebar switches to the Parcelis mark while preserving access to navigation and theme controls. The
organization selector and avatar are available when the sidebar is expanded.
