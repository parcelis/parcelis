id: notifications
title: Notifications
---

Parcelis confirms completed actions with a notification at the top of the page. You will see a confirmation after
creating, updating, archiving, reactivating, or deleting a property, unit, tenant, maintenance ticket, or invoice.

Use the message to confirm that the action completed before continuing. Validation and request errors remain next to
the relevant form or action when Parcelis needs information or cannot save a change.
