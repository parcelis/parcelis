id: profile
title: Profile
---

Select **My Account** at the bottom of the sidebar, then choose **My Profile**. You can update your own name and
phone number. Organization administrators can also update these details from a user's profile page.

Use the **Account info** card to change your sign-in email address or password. Changing your email address requires
your current password. To change your password, enter your current password, then enter and re-enter a new password
of at least 12 characters.

Use the profile photo panel to upload, replace, or remove a GIF, JPG, PNG, or WebP image. Your profile photo is
stored with the active organization for future use alongside your activity and notes.
