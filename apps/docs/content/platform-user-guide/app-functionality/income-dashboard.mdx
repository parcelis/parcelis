title: Income Dashboard
---

Use **Income** in the sidebar to review rent income across the portfolio.

The dashboard summarizes scheduled monthly rent, expected income after overdue balances, and the total overdue
balance for active and notice-period leases.

Use the search field to find income records by property, unit, or lease status.

## Manual Invoices

Select **New Invoice** to add a charge for an existing lease. Choose the property, unit, and lease term; the linked
tenant is filled in automatically. Add one or more charge lines with an item, optional description, quantity, and
rate. The form shows the subtotal, paid amount, and balance due before the invoice is created.

## Income by Property

Income is grouped by property by default. Each property row shows its scheduled rent and overdue balance. Select a
property row to expand its active and notice-period leases, including the tenant, unit, scheduled rent, and overdue
amount. Select an invoice row to open that invoice's details.

Expanded rows show the current lease-derived invoice ID, due date, status, amount, and balance. Paid-on, processing,
and paid values remain unavailable until invoice and payment records are added.

Select the **Grouped By Property** toggle to switch to an ungrouped rent-roll view. This lists each lease with its property, unit,
lease status, scheduled rent, and overdue amount.
