title: Tenant Details
---

Open a tenant detail page by selecting the tenant's name or **View** from the tenant directory.

## Summary Header

The header provides a **Tenants** back action and an **Edit tenant** action. The tenant summary shows:

- Tenant image or a default avatar.
- Name, email address, and phone number.
- Account status.
- Insurance status.
- Lease-derived tenant status.

When an image is available, select the avatar to open a larger preview.

## Lease Summary

The metric cards show the current property and unit, lease dates, monthly rent, overdue balance, and the number of
lease records. A lease without an end date is shown as **Month-to-Month**.

## Contact Information

The Contact Information card shows the tenant's name, email address, phone number, archived date when applicable,
and primary emergency contact. Select the emergency contact's name to review or edit their information.

## Collections and Invoices

The Collection card summarizes current and past-due invoices and collected rent when lease data exists. Use
**View All Invoices** or an invoice identifier to open the corresponding invoice view.

## Lease History

Lease History lists each property, unit, lease term, monthly rent, and lease status. Select a property name to open
its property detail page.
