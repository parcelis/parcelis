title: Sign In
---

Parcelis requires an account before you can access the workspace.

1. Open the sign-in page.
2. Enter your email address and password.
3. Select **Sign in**.

New users can select **Create account** on the same page. Parcelis sends a verification link to the supplied email
address; opening it activates the account before sign-in. Passwords must be at least 12 characters long.
If the link expires, try signing in and select **Resend verification email**.

If sign-in is temporarily unavailable, the login page shows an error and keeps the form open. Contact your
administrator so they can check the authentication service configuration.

For local development, `pnpm db:seed` creates the administrator using `SEED_ADMIN_EMAIL`, which
defaults to `admin@parcelis.dev`, and `SEED_ADMIN_PASSWORD`. Set the password to a unique value of
at least 12 characters before running the seed command. Later seed runs do not replace the
administrator password.

Sign out is available from the **My Account** menu at the bottom of the sidebar. Signing out clears this browser's session cookie and signs out other open tabs in the same browser.

Sessions use a seven-day lifetime that NextAuth can renew when checking the session. There is no inactivity timeout. If your session expires, sign in again.

## Reset a forgotten password

Select **Forgot your password?** beneath the sign-in button, then enter your email address. If an active account uses
that address, Parcelis sends a one-time reset link that expires after 30 minutes. Open the link, choose and confirm a
new password of at least 12 characters, then sign in with the new password. Resetting a password does not sign out other browsers; their sessions remain valid until expiration.

## Change your password

Open **My Account** and select **Change password**. Enter your current password, then enter and re-enter a new
password of at least 12 characters. The new password entries must match before the update can be submitted. You can
also change your password from the **Account info** card on **My Profile**.
