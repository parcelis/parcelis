title: Sign In
---

Parcelis requires an account before you can access the workspace.

1. Open the sign-in page.
2. Enter your email address and password.
3. Select **Sign in**.

New users can select **Create account** on the same page. Passwords must be at least 12 characters long.

For local development, `pnpm db:seed` creates `admin@parcelis.dev` using the value of
`SEED_ADMIN_PASSWORD`. Set this environment variable to a unique password of at least 12 characters
before running the seed command. Later seed runs do not replace the administrator password.

Sign out is available from the **My Account** menu at the bottom of the sidebar. Signing out revokes the current
browser session.

## Reset a forgotten password

Select **Forgot your password?** beneath the sign-in button, then enter your email address. If an active account uses
that address, Parcelis sends a one-time reset link that expires after 30 minutes. Open the link, choose and confirm a
new password of at least 12 characters, then sign in with the new password. Resetting a password signs the account
out of every browser session.

## Change your password

Open **My Account** and select **Change password**. Enter your current password, then enter and re-enter a new
password of at least 12 characters. The new password entries must match before the update can be submitted. You can
also change your password from the **Account info** card on **My Profile**.

## Development Only

Set `AUTH_DISABLED=true` in your local environment to bypass sign-in while developing. This setting is ignored when `NODE_ENV=production`.
