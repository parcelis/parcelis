---

A tenant is a person connected to current or past lease records in Parcelis. Tenant records store contact
information, an optional emergency contact and image, account and insurance statuses, internal notes, and lease history.

## Tenant Status

Parcelis derives tenant status from the tenant's lease and archive records:

- **Active**: The tenant has an active lease or a lease in notice.
- **Past**: The tenant has no active lease or lease in notice.
- **Archived**: The tenant record has been archived.

Tenant status is separate from account status and insurance status.
