Parcelis helps property teams manage properties, units, tenants, and leases from a single workspace. Use this guide
to understand the platform, set up portfolio records, and work through day-to-day operations.

Start with **Getting Started**, then use **Core Concepts** to understand the records in Parcelis. **App
Functionality** contains detailed workflow guides for adding, finding, managing, and reviewing properties and
tenants.

## Current Product Scope

The current platform supports a portfolio dashboard; property and tenant creation, editing, search, and filters;
archive, restore, and delete actions; property and tenant images; emergency contacts; and unit, lease, maintenance,
invoice, collection, and financial summaries when data exists. Notes are available for properties, units, and
tenants.

Portfolio, Properties, and Tenants are active standalone areas. The sidebar also shows planned areas for Leases,
Maintenance, Accounting, and Settings.
